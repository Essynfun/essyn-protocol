# Essyn Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

Builders on Solana leave their history scattered across keys that nothing connects. Essyn exists to put that history under one roof: a single, readable page for each builder, assembled from what the ledger shows rather than from what anyone claims.

Our mission is to make a builder's output easy to find and easy to judge, and to let reputation follow the person instead of the key.

## How it works

1. A builder reserves a handle.
2. Each key the builder holds proves ownership with a single message. Keys can be hidden.
3. Essyn locates what those keys released, orders it by highest valuation, and calculates a score from ledger activity.

No step moves funds.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Public site, contract slot, brand and documentation. Handles can be reserved in principle; nothing is issued.

**Phase 2: Linking.** Message-based key proof, hidden keys, and the first assembled pages.

**Phase 3: Standings.** Score, windows, and the ordered table, all derived from ledger data.

**Phase 4: Reach.** Release alerts, statements and the concept board.
